# Practitioner Evidence: Methods & Acquisition Strategy

## Goal

I wanted to find out what practitioners (consultants, business leaders, and industry experts) think about:

1. What problems come with fully remote work (like isolation, weak culture, and coordination issues)
2. Whether switching to hybrid actually improves retention and employee experience without hurting performance

## Where I looked

### McKinsey pieces on hybrid work, return-to-office, and flexible work
- mckinsey.com
- Their articles and research reports on hybrid work models
- Their analysis of return-to-office trends and policies
- Their research on flexible work arrangements

McKinsey is a major management consulting firm, so their reports focus on what companies are actually doing and what seems to work.

### Gallup work-trend and hybrid work reports
- Gallup.com
- Their work-trend surveys and reports
- Their hybrid work engagement studies
- Their employee wellbeing and thriving research

Gallup runs large national surveys, so they have data on what thousands of workers are experiencing.

### Microsoft Work Trend Index & hybrid guides
- Microsoft's global survey data and their own internal practices
- Their Work Trend Index reports
- Their hybrid work implementation guides
- Their research that combines employee surveys with actual company practices

Microsoft is interesting because they're both studying hybrid work and implementing it at their own company.

### News and company examples of return-to-office backlash
- Coverage of Amazon, Apple, Google, Washington Post, federal workers
- Business Insider articles on return-to-office policies
- The Verge coverage of employee reactions
- Analysis of employee protests and pushback

These news articles show what happens when companies try to bring people back - sometimes it works, sometimes employees push back hard.

## Search strategy

### Keywords I used
- "hybrid work retention McKinsey"
- "Gallup hybrid engagement vs remote"
- "employee reactions return to office mandate"
- "Microsoft Work Trend Index hybrid"

### How I filtered results
- Only looked at post-2020 publications (since that's when hybrid work became mainstream)
- Focused on knowledge workers and remote-capable roles (not jobs like retail or manufacturing that can't be done remotely)

## Getting multiple perspectives

### Management consultancies (McKinsey)
These sources gave me insights on:
- What trade-offs companies face when choosing between work models
- How hybrid vs remote vs in-office affects productivity
- Real estate and operational issues companies need to think about
- Frameworks for making strategic decisions

### Survey organizations (Gallup, Microsoft)
These sources gave me:
- Survey data from thousands of employees on engagement levels
- Data on wellbeing and whether people are "thriving"
- What employees prefer, broken down by demographics
- Trends over time in work arrangements

### Big-tech case examples (Apple, Amazon, Google, federal government)
These sources showed me:
- How employees actually reacted when companies changed their policies
- Examples of employee protests, internal letters, and organized resistance
- What leaders said to justify their decisions
- What worked and what didn't in different implementations
